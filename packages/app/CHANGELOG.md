# @dm-hero/app

## 1.22.0

### Minor Changes

- Roster: separate named rosters — one per city or region — as tabs. Add a new roster any time, rename or delete one (its places move to another roster), and move a place to another roster from its menu. Your current roster becomes "Main roster".
- Dashboard: the Reputation card lists everyone A to Z, split into people, factions and places, with a search box and photos. The NPCs card gets a search box that finds any NPC in the campaign.
- Story board: the storyline filter and the color legend fold up to one line (the storylines you've picked stay visible); remembered per campaign.
- Story map: cluster cards show a plain "Add to next session" button under the title (it was a small calendar icon in the corner).
- Storylines: drag to reorder them in Manage storylines; the color picker opens on the storyline's current color.
- Stat blocks from notes: "Multiattack" counts as an action, and the stat block stops at the first line that isn't part of it, so rules and notes below it stay in the notes.

## 1.21.0

### Minor Changes

- Story map: a calendar button on cluster (and beat) cards puts the whole cluster into the next session — every beat not played, cut or archived — or takes it back out. Clusters with beats in the tray show under the Next session filter. The same option sits in a cluster's menu on the board.
- Story map: a cluster's beats are clickable — each one opens up to show its text, place and who's involved, with a shortcut to edit it.
- Storyline colors: pick any color for a storyline, save colors as presets, add and delete your own presets, and hide theme colors you don't use.
- Story board: show beats by status — Idea, Ready, Played, Cut — in any mix (replaces "Hide played"); remembered per campaign.

## 1.20.0

### Minor Changes

- Monsters: a new page with the official 5e creatures (SRD, about 330 — goblins to dragons) ready to use, plus your own. Search by name or type, filter by CR and official/homebrew, copy an official one to make your own version.
- Stat blocks that read at a glance: AC/HP/speed/CR pills, the six abilities, then traits, actions, reactions and legendary actions. Click an attack's "+4 to hit" or "1d6+2 slashing" to roll it, an ability to roll a check, the hit dice to roll HP — the result shows big (crits green, ones red).
- NPCs get the same stat block in their side panel. Stats already written in an NPC's notes ("AC 16 - HP 22 …", "**Spear:** +4 to hit …") can be read in with one click — check them, save, and the stat lines leave the notes (the original notes are kept as a backup). Or paste a stat block in anywhere.
- Encounters: add monsters straight from the library — how many, average or rolled HP; each rolls its own initiative. NPCs with a stat block can join even without a stat template.
- Combat: whoever's turn it is shows their full stat block beside the HP controls, with click-to-roll attacks; one-click conditions (Prone, Grappled, Poisoned, Concentrating …) that light up and show as icons on the turn tiles.

## 1.0.0

The first stable release of DM Hero - a personal D&D campaign management tool for Dungeon Masters.

### Core Features

- **Entity Management**: NPCs, Locations, Items, Factions, Lore, Players, Sessions
- **Fuzzy Search**: FTS5 + Levenshtein across all fields including linked entities
- **Bidirectional Relations**: NPCs know Lore, Lore shows NPCs
- **Entity Linking**: `{{npc:123}}` in Markdown for clickable badges with preview
- **Rich Markdown Editor**: Full formatting with entity links and syntax highlighting

### Campaign Tools

- **In-Game Calendar**: Configurable months, seasons, weather per day
- **Interactive Maps**: Upload world maps, place markers, link to entities
- **Chaos Graph**: Force-directed visualization of entity relationships
- **Session Management**: Track sessions with dates, notes, audio recordings

### AI Integration

- **DALL-E Image Generation**: AI portraits for entities and session covers
- **GPT Name Generation**: Context-aware name suggestions

### Platform Support

- **Desktop**: Windows (EXE/Installer) and Linux (AppImage) via Electron
- **Web/Docker**: Self-hosted deployment with SQLite database
- **Auto-Updates**: Built-in update checker with download notifications

### User Experience

- **Dark & Light Themes**: Fantasy-themed color schemes
- **i18n**: German and English language support
- **Local-First**: All data stored locally in SQLite
- **Export/Import**: Full campaign backup and sharing
